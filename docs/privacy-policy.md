# AI Launcher privacy policy

*Effective 25 November 2026*

AI Launcher is an Android home-screen app with a built-in AI assistant called Aura. The assistant's
AI model runs **on your phone**. This policy explains what the app uses, why, and the few cases
where anything leaves your device. "We" means the developer of AI Launcher.

## The short version

- Your conversations with the assistant, and everything it works with (your apps, calendar,
  contacts, files, notifications, to-dos), are processed **on your phone**. We do not receive them.
- The Google Play version has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers**, and we run no
  servers that collect your data.
- Something leaves your phone only when you start it: downloading an AI model, sending a report
  about an AI reply, or asking another app to do something (open a link, share a file, place a
  call).

## What the app uses on your phone, and why

Everything in this section stays on your device. Optional permissions are requested only when you
use the feature that needs them, and you can withdraw them at any time in Android Settings.

- **Installed apps.** To show your app drawer, search, folders and suggestions.
- **App usage** (optional "Usage access"). To find apps you haven't used for a while and to suggest
  apps you are likely to open.
- **Calendar** (optional). To include today's events in your agenda and day planner, and to add
  reminders you ask for. This covers every calendar synced to your phone (Google, Outlook,
  Exchange, Samsung and others); you can choose which ones the app reads.
- **Photos you pick** (optional). If you choose a photo as the chat background, the app copies
  that one photo, picked with Android's photo picker, into its private storage. It needs no
  storage permission and sees no other photos.
- **Contacts** (optional). To find the person when you ask the assistant to call or message
  someone. Nothing is called or sent until you confirm, and the call or message is made by your
  phone or messaging app.
- **Notifications** (optional notification digest, off by default). If you turn it on, the app
  reads the title and text of notifications you receive to show unread badges, one-tap codes,
  meeting links and parcel tracking, and to answer "what did I miss?". This data is stored only on
  your phone, deleted automatically after 24 hours, and deleted immediately if you turn the digest
  off.
- **Files.** In the Google Play version, the file tools (largest files, old files, recent changes)
  only look inside folders you pick with Android's folder picker; you can remove them in Settings.
  In the version installed outside Google Play, you can instead allow access to photos, videos,
  audio and, optionally, all files.
- **Accessibility service** (optional, "AI Launcher gestures"). Used only to turn the screen off
  when you double-tap the home screen and to open the notification shade when you swipe down. It
  does not read, collect or share anything shown on your screen.
- **Device admin** (optional). Only to turn the screen off, as an alternative to the accessibility
  service.
- **Phone calls.** When you confirm a call request, to place the call directly; without this
  permission the dialer opens instead.
- **Biometrics.** To unlock Private Space, where hidden apps are kept. Fingerprint and face data
  are handled by Android; the app never sees them.
- **Voice input.** Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech-recognition app (the app
  asks it to work offline where possible); that app's own privacy policy applies.

## Data stored on your phone

Assistant chats, to-dos, planner blocks, folders, your home-screen layout, settings (including the
assistant's name and the instructions you give it), app categories, subscribed calendars, the chat
background photo and the notification digest are kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android. You can
clear the chat history in Settings, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If you use
Android's own backup, it may save your launcher settings to your Google account; chats, to-dos,
notifications and AI models are excluded. A phone-to-phone transfer that you start copies
everything.

## What leaves your phone

- **Downloading an AI model.** The app downloads the Qwen3 model files from Hugging Face
  (huggingface.co). As with any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address and standard
  connection information; see [huggingface.co/privacy](https://huggingface.co/privacy). The app
  sends no personal information with the download.
- **Reports about AI replies.** If you tap **Report** under a reply and then **Send report**, the
  app sends us that reply, the reason you chose, any details you typed, your question (unless you
  untick "Include my question") and the app and model version. Nothing else is included. Depending
  on the version, the report is sent over HTTPS to a form we use for this purpose, or your email app
  opens with the report filled in for you to send. We use reports only to make the assistant safer,
  keep them only as long as needed to review them, and delete one on request.
- **Calendar links you add (only in the version installed outside Google Play, for now).** If you
  subscribe to a calendar by link (an .ics or webcal address, for example a published Outlook.com
  calendar), the app downloads that calendar from the address you entered and refreshes it every
  few hours. Like any download, the calendar's server receives your IP address and the request for
  that address; nothing else is sent. The events are stored only on your phone, and removing the
  calendar deletes them.
- **Things you ask another app to do.** Opening a link, sharing or opening a file, calling or
  messaging someone, or setting an alarm hands that request to the app you choose (browser, phone,
  messaging app, clock). Those apps' own policies apply.
- **Optional cloud backup (only in the version installed outside Google Play).** If you sign in
  with Google and turn on cloud backup, a backup of your settings, dock, hidden apps and app
  categories is stored in Google Firebase. It is encrypted on your phone with a passphrase only you
  know before it is uploaded, so we cannot read it. The Google Play version has no sign-in or cloud
  backup.

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

## Children

AI Launcher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from
them.

## Security

Data the app stores stays in its private storage, which other apps cannot read. Private Space needs
your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

## Your choices

- Withdraw any optional permission or special access in Android Settings.
- Turn off the notification digest to delete its data.
- Clear the assistant's chat history in Settings, or uninstall the app to delete everything.
- Contact us to ask about, or delete, a report you sent.
