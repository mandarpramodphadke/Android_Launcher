## Full description

AI Launcher is a fast, clean home screen with a built-in AI assistant, Aura, that runs entirely on
your phone. Your questions and the things the assistant helps with never leave your device: no
account, no ads, no tracking.

AN ASSISTANT THAT RESPECTS YOUR PRIVACY
• Runs on-device with Qwen3 models (0.6B, 1.7B or 4B). It works offline once the model is downloaded.
• Set alarms, timers and reminders, open apps and folders, and toggle the flashlight or Do Not
  Disturb, just by asking.
• "Summarize my day": calendar events, reminders, your plan and urgent to-dos in one answer.
• Manage to-dos and plan your day in time blocks, by chat or on the built-in planner screen.
• Find large or forgotten files in folders you choose, and apps you haven't opened in weeks.
• Call or message a contact by name, always with a confirmation first.
• Optional notification digest: one-tap codes, meeting links and parcel tracking, plus "what did I
  miss?". It stays on your phone and is deleted after 24 hours.
• Share text or a PDF to AI Launcher to summarize it or pull out tasks.
• Optional GPU acceleration (Vulkan) on supported phones.

A HOME SCREEN THAT STAYS OUT OF YOUR WAY
• Swipeable pages with apps, folders and widgets on a 4- or 5-column grid.
• An app drawer you pull up with your finger, with A–Z, AI-sorted categories and your folders.
• Five suggested apps based on your habits, the time of day and your headphones.
• Folders with a purpose, such as "Work: apps for my job", on any page or in the dock.
• Private Space: hide apps behind your fingerprint, face or PIN.
• Six themes, including AMOLED black, Colourful and Neon, plus a text-only minimal mode.
• Themed icons, double-tap to lock, swipe down for notifications.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The AI model runs on your phone. AI Launcher has no servers that receive your chats, contacts,
calendar, notifications or files. The only things that ever leave your phone are the model
download itself and a report about an AI reply, if you choose to send one.

Good to know
• The assistant can make mistakes. Use Report under any reply to flag something inappropriate.
• The recommended model needs about 1.1 GB of storage and a phone with 4 GB of RAM or more.
• Accessibility service use: the optional "AI Launcher gestures" service is used only to lock the
  screen when you double-tap and to open the notification shade when you swipe down. It never
  reads your screen.
